SCENARIO: Creating 50 Spanish-speaking bots
────────────────────────────────────────────

1. Open Create Bots Dialog

2. Configure:
   Number of Bots: 50
   Language: Spanish
   
   Role Distribution:
   • Warrior: 70%  (35 bots)
   • Merchant: 20% (10 bots)
   • Friendly: 10% (5 bots)

3. Click "Create Bots"

4. Result in IA_Accounts.xml:
   
   Bot0001-Bot0035: BotRole="0" (Warriors)
     • Combat enabled
     • All hunter settings
     • SkillID active
   
   Bot0036-Bot0045: BotRole="1" (Merchants)
     • SkillID="-1"
     • TuNhatItem="1" (picks jewels!)
     • MoveRange="50"
     • Will walk in safe zones
   
   Bot0046-Bot0050: BotRole="2" (Friendly)
     • SkillID="-1"
     • PartyMode="2" (sends invites!)
     • MoveRange="40"
     • Will help newbies

5. In-Game Experience:
   
   WARRIORS (Bot0001-0035):
   • Hunt in dungeons
   • Say: "Buen día a todos"
   • Do PVP
   
   MERCHANTS (Bot0036-0045):
   • Walk in Lorencia
   • Say: "Cambio joyas aquí!"
   • Trade with players
   
   FRIENDLY (Bot0046-0050):
   • Walk in newbie areas
   • Say: "¿Necesitas ayuda?"
   • Invite low-levels to party
